GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got you.

Everything you need to know before the party starts.

Is PartyPrint really free?

Yes. You can make, print and save your party games without paying or making an account. Optional extras may be offered in the future; the core tools will remain free to use.

How do I download a PDF?

Choose Letter or A4 and tap “Download PDF.” On a computer, tap “Save PDF.” On iPhone or iPad, tap “Save to Files,” then choose Save to Files in the share sheet. “Share PDF” opens the same sheet. If file sharing is unavailable, open the PDF and use the viewer’s Share menu to save it. Use “Print games” for browser printing.

Will everyone get the same bingo card?

No. The bingo generator uses a different shuffled arrangement for each card. Print one per guest, up to 48 cards per pack.

Do you store my party details?

No account or server-side storage is used. Your entries are processed in your browser. A shared setup link includes only the game choices, not names or custom prompts. See our privacy page.

Can I use these at school or work?

Of course. These games are free for personal, classroom and internal workplace events. Please don't resell the generated designs as your own products.
