THE SIMPLE RULES

Terms of use.

Last updated: October 12, 2026. PartyPrint is offered as a free tool for making printable party games. By using it, you agree to the guidelines below.

Allowed use

You may generate, download via your browser's print dialog, print and share the completed game sheets for personal celebrations, classroom activities and internal workplace events. You can use them at events you organize, including events that charge for attendance, as long as you are not selling PartyPrint's generated templates as standalone products.

Not allowed

Please don't copy our website, bulk-repackage generated designs for resale, claim the PartyPrint brand as your own, or use the service in a way that interferes with other visitors. Do not put private or harmful material into cards intended for others.

Your content

You decide what to type into event titles and custom prompts. Because these are included on your printed cards, only enter content you have permission to share with your guests. The browser-based app doesn't require you to upload that content to our servers.

Availability and accuracy

We work to keep the game maker usable and questions accurate, but it is provided as-is, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability or error-free output. Check your print preview and proofread personal details before printing. Some printers and browser print dialogs may format pages differently.

Updates and feedback

We may improve games, change site features or update these terms. Updates will be reflected on this page. To report a problem, use the project issue tracker and avoid sharing sensitive details publicly.
