THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Your party, your privacy.

Last updated: October 12, 2026. We want this site to be useful without collecting your party details.

What the game maker does

The generator runs in your browser. PDF files are also created on your device; party titles, custom words and generated documents are not uploaded. The export engine and font are downloaded from PartyPrint only when needed. Files remain in memory until you edit your pack, close or reload the page; saving or sharing the actual PDF is your choice. Your party title, custom prompts, personal quiz questions and host answers are used to display and print the pages on your device. They are not sent by the game maker to a PartyPrint server, and you don't need an account. The separate Print games option uses your browser's built-in print dialog.

Photo-inspired colors

If you choose a photo, your browser reads it locally to suggest printable colors. The image, filename and metadata are not uploaded or stored by PartyPrint. The photo is not printed or included in shared setup links, and the extracted colors remain only while this page is open. This matches colors rather than recreating the photo’s artwork.

Share links

If you choose “Share this setup,” the URL contains generic settings: occasion, theme, card count, game selections, bingo size and baby shower bingo style. It does not include the host's name, the party title, custom bingo prompts, or personal quiz questions and answers. Whoever receives the URL can see the settings included in that link.

Guest links and playing on phones

The separate “Create guest link & QR” option intentionally includes your party title, selected games, custom bingo words and personal quiz questions in the link. Personal host answers and photos are excluded. Anyone with the link can read the included game text; it is not a secret or encrypted invitation. Do not include sensitive information. The game settings are carried in a URL fragment, which browsers do not send as part of the web request. QR codes are generated locally, without a remote QR service.

Guest answers, marks, choices and drawings are stored in that browser’s local storage to survive a reload. They are not uploaded or submitted to the host. Guests can use “Clear my answers and marks” or clear the browser’s site data to remove them. Storage may be unavailable in some browsing modes. Guest play pages do not load our analytics beacon. Links represent a snapshot: editing the host kit requires a new link. There is no live room or scoreboard.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

PartyPrint uses Cloudflare Web Analytics on the production website to measure page views, approximate visits, referral sources, browser and device categories, and page performance. Cloudflare describes this service as privacy-first and cookie-free. We do not send your party title, custom prompts or identity as analytics events. Generator, print and share actions are not separately tracked, and a print-dialog opening does not prove a successful print. See Cloudflare Web Analytics information. We do not use advertising scripts, tracking cookies or third-party fonts. Any future advertising will require an updated policy and applicable consent handling.

Website hosting

The infrastructure provider hosting the website may process necessary technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request timestamps and error logs to serve and protect the site. Its practices are governed by the hosting provider's own privacy terms. If the site is hosted using Cloudflare, see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

External links

Some links may take you to GitHub for feedback or to other external websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Clicking an external link is your choice.

Questions and changes

You can raise a general privacy question through our project issue tracker. Please don't post personal details there because GitHub issues may be public. We'll update this page if our practices change.
