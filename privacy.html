THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Your party, your privacy.

Last updated: October 12, 2026. We want this site to be useful without collecting your party details.

What the game maker does

The generator runs in your browser. Your party title and custom prompts are used to display and print the pages on your device. They are not sent by the game maker to a PartyPrint server, and you don't need an account. Printing and saving PDFs use your browser's built-in print dialog.

Share links

If you choose “Share this setup,” the URL contains generic settings: occasion, theme, card count, game selections and bingo size. It does not include the host's name, the party title or the custom bingo prompts. Whoever receives the URL can see the settings included in that link.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

As launched, PartyPrint does not include advertising scripts, behavioral analytics, tracking cookies or third-party fonts. If advertising or analytics is introduced, this policy will be revised before those tools are enabled, and any necessary consent mechanism will be added. This is not a claim that your web browser or host never processes technical data.

Website hosting

The infrastructure provider hosting the website may process necessary technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request timestamps and error logs to serve and protect the site. Its practices are governed by the hosting provider's own privacy terms. If the site is hosted using Cloudflare, see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

External links

Some links may take you to GitHub for feedback or to other external websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Clicking an external link is your choice.

Questions and changes

You can raise a general privacy question through our project issue tracker. Please don't post personal details there because GitHub issues may be public. We'll update this page if our practices change.
