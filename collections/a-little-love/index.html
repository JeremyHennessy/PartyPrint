A LITTLE LOVE / BABY SHOWER COLLECTION

Little wishes.

A coordinated sample to bring guests together and give the family something to treasure. Six pages, free to download. No account or checkout.

Download Letter PDF ↓ Download A4 PDF ↓

What is inside?

  • A cover and practical host plan.
  • Baby prediction cards: two guests per sheet.
  • Words of encouragement: two keepsake cards per sheet.
  • A private gift record for thank-you notes.
  • A five-minute baby-name alphabet activity.

This is a free design sample, not a paid product. It complements the generator; bingo and trivia are created separately using the link below.

How to use the collection

  1. Choose Letter for 8.5 × 11 inch paper or A4 for 210 × 297 mm paper.
  2. Print at actual size or 100%. Leave browser headers and footers off.
  3. Print enough prediction and encouragement pages for your guests. Cut at the dotted center line.
  4. Offer pens and invite guests to participate. Names and personal details are optional.
  5. Keep the completed notes as keepsakes. Keep gift and guest details private.

Use plain paper for activities or light card stock for keepsakes if your printer supports it. The layouts have mostly white backgrounds and work without borderless printing. Physical-printer results have not yet been independently tested.
